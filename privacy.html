StewardOS privacy policy

Personal prototype. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What StewardOS is

StewardOS is a personal prototype for its owner: a place to keep their own desk, calendar, and related notes. This page covers the Google sign-in used when that owner connects Google Calendar. The prototype is maintained by the StewardOS developer / project owner.

What Google access covers

When the owner connects Google, StewardOS asks for permission to read and write Google Calendar for the account that signs in. That includes the calendar events Google returns for that account, and the ability to add or change events during a sync.

Calendar data is used only to sync with StewardOS, at the owner’s request. StewardOS does not sell personal data.

Where credentials stay

Google OAuth credentials and the tokens Google issues stay on the owner’s own machine or server, with the owner’s copy of the app.

Revoking Google access

The owner can disconnect StewardOS at any time. Open Google Account third-party access, select StewardOS, and remove access. After that, StewardOS can no longer read or change that Google Calendar. Events already saved on the owner’s machine or server stay there until the owner deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the StewardOS developer / project owner. This prototype does not publish a separate support address.
